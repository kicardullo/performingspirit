Connections between the worlds of theatre, mesmerism, and spiritualism are well-known. While séances were performed publicly and privately by both believers and charlatans, mediums worldwide were dedicated to proving the existence of spirits- at any cost. This virtual exhibition compiles several of the tools and products of séances in the context of their use as vehicles for the suspension of disbelief.
<br>
NOTE: Research for this exhibit has been loosely conducted from several books and general searches, but is NOT a part of an academic research project. <br>
Works consulted include:<br>
"Supernatural Entertainments: Victorian Spiritualism and the Rise of Modern Media Culture" by Simone Natale<br>
"Ghosts and things: the Material Culture of Nineteenth-Century Spiritualism" by Aviva Briefel<br>
"Singer in the Shadows: The Strange Story of Patience Worth" by Irving Litvag<br>
"The Spirit World Unmasked: Illustrated Investigations Into the Phenomena of Spiritualism and Theosophy" by Henry Ridgely Evans<br>
